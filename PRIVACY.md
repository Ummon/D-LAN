# D-LAN Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

D-LAN is a free, open-source application for sharing files and chatting on a local area network (LAN). This policy explains what information D-LAN handles and where it goes.


## Summary

D-LAN does not collect any personal data for its author. It has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. D-LAN does not send any data to the internet.


## Information shared with other computers on your network

D-LAN only works by communicating with other D-LAN instances on your local network. Other D-LAN users on the same network can see:

- Your nickname (chosen by you) and a randomly generated peer identifier.
- Your computer's IP address.
- The names, sizes and content hashes of the files and folders you choose to share, and the contents of those files when someone downloads them.
- The chat messages you send.

Only the folders you explicitly add to your shares are visible. You can stop sharing a folder at any time in the settings.


## Information stored on your computer

D-LAN stores the following locally, in your user data folder:

- Your settings (nickname, shared folders, network settings).
- A cache of file hashes for the shared folders.
- Recent chat messages.
- Log files used for troubleshooting.
- If you enable remote control, a salted hash of the remote password.

This data never leaves your computer except as described above. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version of D-LAN removes it.


## Links

Clicking a link in a chat message, or the link in the About dialog, opens it in your web browser. The browser and the website you visit apply their own privacy policies.


## Microsoft Store

When you install D-LAN from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect installation and usage data under its own privacy statement: <https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement>. The author of D-LAN receives only the aggregated, anonymous statistics Microsoft provides to all publishers.


## Children

D-LAN does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
